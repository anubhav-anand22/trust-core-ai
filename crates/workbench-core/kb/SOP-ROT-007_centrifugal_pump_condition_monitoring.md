# SOP-ROT-007 — Centrifugal Pump Condition Monitoring and Leak Response

**Revision:** 6 | **Owner:** Rotating Equipment | **Applies to:** All API 610 centrifugal pumps in hydrocarbon and hot-water service.

## 1. Routine operator checks (per shift)
- Mechanical seal: no visible drip. A seal in acceptable condition shows ≤ 5 drops/min of clear fluid; > 20 drops/min or any product stream requires a notification.
- Bearing housing temperature: ≤ 80 °C, or ≤ 40 °C above ambient, whichever is lower.
- Vibration (overall, unfiltered): alert at 4.5 mm/s RMS, trip / withdraw from service at 7.1 mm/s RMS (ISO 10816-3, medium machines, flexible support).
- Baseplate / hold-down bolts: no cracking of grout, no fretting at pump feet.
- Suction strainer ΔP within normal band; no cavitation noise ("gravel" sound).

## 2. Seal leak classification

| Class | Observation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Occasional weep, clear fluid, ≤ 5 drops/min | Monitor, log, trend. |
| S2 | Steady clear drip 5–20 drops/min | Plan seal replacement at next opportunity; increase check frequency to hourly. |
| S3 | Product visible, > 20 drops/min, or any atomized/spraying leak | Notify shift supervisor. Start spare pump, isolate and de-pressure the leaking pump. |
| S4 | Running leak forming a pool, smoking gland, or seal fire | Emergency stop, activate spare, foam/steam cover as per fire plan, evacuate immediate area. |

## 3. Valve and flange leaks near pumps
Any leak at a valve gland or flange in the pump suction/discharge line is treated at the same class table as seal leaks (S1–S4) by leak rate and whether product is visible.

## 4. Corrosion on pump externals
Refer external metal loss on the casing, supports, or small-bore connections to **SOP-COR-014**. Corrosion at a casing drain or seal-flush connection is graded one level higher because of the containment consequence.

## 5. Records
Pump tag, parameter readings, leak class, photograph, action taken, operator, timestamp. File against the pump's condition-monitoring history.
