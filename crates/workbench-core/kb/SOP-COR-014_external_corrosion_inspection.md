# SOP-COR-014 — External Corrosion Inspection of Piping and Pumps

**Revision:** 4 | **Owner:** Inspection (Static Equipment) | **Applies to:** Units 100–400 process piping, pump casings, and associated small-bore connections.

## 1. Purpose
Define the acceptance criteria and required actions when external corrosion, coating breakdown, or metal loss is identified during a routine or for-cause visual inspection.

## 2. Corrosion severity grading

| Grade | Visual description | Action |
|---|---|---|
| C1 – Light | Surface discoloration or blistered coating, no measurable metal loss | Log in register. Re-inspect at next scheduled interval. |
| C2 – Moderate | Coating loss with early scaling / flaky rust, pitting < 1.0 mm deep | Clean to bare metal, UT thickness check within 30 days, schedule recoat. |
| C3 – Severe | Laminar scale, pitting 1.0–3.0 mm, or metal loss over a patch ≥ 50 mm | Raise a priority notification. UT/pit-gauge within 72 h. Engineering fitness-for-service assessment before continued service. |
| C4 – Critical | Through-wall risk, weeping product, scale over welds or supports, or pitting > 3.0 mm | Immediate notification to shift supervisor. Consider clamp / rate reduction / shutdown. Do not defer. |

## 3. Special locations (inspect closely, grade one level higher if in doubt)
- Pipe supports and contact points (crevice / under-lagging corrosion).
- Pump casing drains, vent bosses, and seal-flush small-bore lines.
- Dead legs and intermittent-flow lines.
- Insulation damage or missing cladding — treat as suspected CUI (corrosion under insulation).

## 4. Required record for every finding
Equipment tag, location on component, severity grade, photograph, measured pit depth if taken, inspector, date.

## 5. Safety
Product-wetted external corrosion (any weeping, staining, or hydrocarbon smell) is a loss-of-containment precursor — stop, secure the area, and notify the shift supervisor before further handling.
